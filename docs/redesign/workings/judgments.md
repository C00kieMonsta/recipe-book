# Judge: dalia

Ranking rationale from Dalia's seat: Proposal 0 is the only one whose home screen, day sheet and prep sheet are described down to what fits on one phone screen and what she taps with wet hands, and it ships the readable recipe first and the status tick from the start. Proposal 4 is the safest to actually ship and the easiest for her to follow, but it leaves the day sheet read-only until week nine. Proposal 1 has the best Notion feel but makes the week wait until phase 3 and keeps hover-editing on the recipe page. Proposal 3 understands the books best (monthly invoicing, MP réel vs calculé, extras) but delivers the journal before anything she complained about. Proposal 2 is a backend spec with thin screens and was penalised for that as instructed.

Code checks: the five proposals agree on the facts and almost all checkable claims hold (h-22, select-none, 16 tiny-text uses, Events.tsx:45 descending sort, -m-6 and DEFAULT_SUPPLIERS in GroceryListPage, estimateBelgianTax and hex colours in Dashboard, unused QueryCommand import, SETTINGS_TTL 120s, EventEditor full-payload autosave at 800ms, addRecipe duplicate guard, handleDuplicate using today's date, unused packages/utils and TABLE_NAMES, no reference check on ingredient delete, five CDK tables, README four-step table procedure, xlsx present, jest project for types). Wrong or unverifiable: Proposal 0's Dashboard sort-mutation claim, Proposal 2's zod 3.25 and its use of tsx (not installed; neither is ts-node, so any script runner needs a dev dependency). All five share the same accounting formulas and the same unconfirmed defaults (atelier at 21 % VAT, 25 % corporate rate, VAT on deposits); whichever plan is chosen, those three questions go to the accountant before the first export.

Common risk none of them solves fully: the settings item is put-based last-write-wins and will hold courses, prestation types, fixed charges and objectives; fine for one user, but worth an expectedUpdatedAt guard once accounting lands.

All five proposals share the same journal shape (14 workbook columns, VAT per rate, paid/method/date received, type, KM, HTVA and TVAC stored separately with an identity refine), the same SRL model (fixed charges with owner salary and social contributions, ISoc on the result, `estimateBelgianTax` deleted), the same record-versus-view split, the same cost-snapshot-at-completion, and the same export format. Shared deviations from the workbook that the final plan must fix, regardless of winner:
1. Breakeven. The template computes seuil annuel = annual charges + 12 × monthly treasury target + investments (`C42 + C47*12 + C48`) and seuil mensuel = monthly charges + monthly treasury target + investments/12 (`C41 + C47 + C48/12`). Every proposal makes an MP-adjusted formula primary (`/(1 − rawMaterialPct)`) and divides the monthly figure by `monthsActive` (10). Both change her numbers. Keep the workbook formula as the primary figure with the same labels, and show the MP-adjusted one as a second line labelled as such. Note that `C47` (treasury target per month) is empty in the live file; the field must default to 0, not to charges × 3.
2. Corporate tax. The template uses 15 %; the proposals default to 25 % with a note about the 20 % SME rate. The rate must be a per-year setting; whichever default is chosen, say on the page that the workbook used 15 % so she recognises the discrepancy.
3. Cost precedence. Proposals 3 and 4 put `costSnapshot` before `actualCost`, which makes 'Coût réel' a dead field after closure and contradicts today's `EventEditor` rule. The final plan must use `actualCost ?? costSnapshot?.rawMaterial ?? live`, as 0, 1 and 2 do.
4. Deposits. Proposals 0 and 1 prefill the final invoice at full price × guests; 2, 3 and 4 subtract journaled acomptes. Use the subtraction. Whether a deposit is a VAT-bearing revenue line in the month received (all five assume yes) must be confirmed with the accountant; the workbook keeps ACOMPTES as a separate block, not journal lines.
5. Multi-event invoices (monthly counter sales, Hoona). Only proposal 3 models `eventIds[]`; it does not say how to attribute the amount per event, so Au réel would count the invoice once per event. Add a pro-rata rule.
6. Proposal 2's `discriminatedUnion` over `.innerType()` drops the HTVA + TVA = TVAC refine; use a union of the refined schemas or refine after the union.
