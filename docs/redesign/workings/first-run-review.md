# First-run review (2026-10-12)

Five first-time personas clicked through the running app (seeded week 2026-W42 and an empty install) at 375 and 1366 wide. A judge merged their 98 findings into the fixes below. The guided tour is specified in [04-first-run-and-tour.md](../04-first-run-and-tour.md).

## What a first-time user struggles with

First-time users struggle most with these, in order:
1. Fresh install: the week offers one action (create an event) that cannot be completed because people, recipes and clients do not exist yet; the event page then shows a bare recipe search with no results and an Équipe menu whose only row is "Personne".
2. Phone sub-pages (Jour, Événement, Fiche, Recette, Client) drop the ☰; the only exit is back. AdminSidebar is owned by the tour builder, so this is handed over, not fixed here.
3. The week stat line ("Signé … · 279 conv. · 1 / 20 lignes faites · Mois … ›") is unreadable on first sight and its link is hover-only.
4. Assigning work: the AM chip looks like a label, unassigned cards show a 20px dashed icon, lines have no click target, "Personne" reads as a person, and the Assigner dialog lists all ten events without saying clicks save at once.
5. The checklist bullets look like checkboxes, the count is a bare "3", and the verbs (Créer, Voir, Assigner) do not say where they go; on phones it is collapsed by default.
6. The day page has no create button, and a cook with no line does not appear at all in "Par personne".
7. On phones the date is shown five times on the day page (top bar, breadcrumb, h1, DateNav, strip).
8. The fiche hides the sidebar with only a 14px grey crumb as exit; the "1/2" pager and the ingredient circles are unexplained; the cook-mode X and Terminer do not say where they go or that Terminer marks the line done.
9. Creating events: "+ Nouveau" does not say what, the phone + has no text, the editor's back link says "Événements", nothing says a client fills the menu, Dupliquer defaults to today.
10. Comptabilité: six Excel-named tabs with no sentence saying what each holds; the ledger form uses "N° (Falco)", "KM", and never says that ticking an event marks it Facturé.
11. Courses empty state tells the user to generate from the week without a link; three screens word the same action differently.
12. Vocabulary: "ligne", "conv.", "évén.", "Signé", "faites" with no noun; "Assigner" and "Attribuer" for one action.
Several deeper problems (income statement charging fixed costs before the journal start, client month invoicing of upcoming events, Prévisionnel/Finances duplication, phone overflow on Aperçu/Résultats) are real but need library or layout work and are deferred.
